# PD Serving Benchmark: 2× RTX 4090

This report is a compact, reviewable extract of a 96-run Standard suite completed
on 2026-07-24 with Qwen3-0.6B and two 24 GB RTX 4090 GPUs. The GPUs communicate
through a cross-NUMA PCIe `SYS` path. Raw request events and telemetry remain
local; the accompanying JSON contains the reviewable aggregate data.

## Method

The open-loop synthetic suite compares four modes: one TP1 instance, TP2 across
two GPUs, two routed TP1 replicas (`local-dp2`), and one-prefill/one-decode PD.
The 75% load point uses five paired repetitions; other load points are useful
observations but have one repetition. SLOs are calibrated per workload and
shared by all modes. Consequently, goodput is an internal workload-specific
metric, not a production SLA.

## Results at 75% Load

| Workload | Mode | P99 ITL (ms) | P95 TTFT (ms) | Output tok/s |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Prefill interference | TP1 | 28.87 | 200.68 | 980.61 |
| Prefill interference | DP2 | 27.16 | 212.63 | 979.99 |
| Prefill interference | PD | 4.46 | 348.52 | 981.03 |
| Mixed chat | TP1 | 38.95 | 118.42 | 1096.56 |
| Mixed chat | DP2 | 27.46 | 124.02 | 1125.80 |
| Mixed chat | PD | 7.86 | 165.13 | 1113.00 |
| Decode heavy | TP1 | 28.78 | 47.94 | 2633.08 |
| Decode heavy | DP2 | 26.92 | 53.23 | 2672.85 |
| Decode heavy | PD | 5.91 | 137.27 | 2573.86 |

PD consistently reduced tail inter-token latency by isolating decode from
prefill work, while KV handoff and fixed P/D resource allocation increased
TTFT. DP2 matched or exceeded PD goodput in several cases, especially for
decode-heavy traffic, because both GPUs can decode. TP2 was inefficient for
this small model over the tested `SYS` topology.

## Interpretation and Limits

These measurements support a latency-isolation claim, not a universal
throughput claim. They do not show that PD beats every replicated deployment,
model size, interconnect, workload, or advanced monolithic scheduler. Chunked
prefill and stall-free scheduling can also reduce decode stalls. Re-run the
suite after performance-sensitive changes and use at least two paired samples
for regression gating.
